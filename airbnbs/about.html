WHY WE STARTED

Hospitality Built On Peace, Freedom & Quality

Founded with a vision to redefine short-term rentals in Kenya, Jirani Stays provides handpicked, beautifully finished serviced apartments and villas across Nairobi’s premier residential neighborhoods: Kilimani, Westlands, Kileleshwa, and Lavington.

Every apartment in our portfolio is personally vetted, deeply sanitized, and equipped with smart digital self check-in, dedicated high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, and 24/7 concierge assistance on WhatsApp. No awkward key handoffs, no surprises — just pure relaxation.
