The Ultimate Local Guide to Nairobi: Where to Stay, Eat, and Explore in 2026

Jan 05, 2026 • 5 min read

Welcome to Nairobi, the only city in the world with a National Park in its backyard. Whether you’re here for a business trip in Westlands or a relaxing getaway in Karen, Jirani Properties has curated the perfect guide to help you navigate the city like a local.

1. Top Neighborhoods for Your Stay

Choosing the right area is key to a great Nairobi experience:

  • Westlands: The heart of Nairobi's nightlife and modern shopping. Stay here for easy access to Sarit Centre and Westgate Mall. View our Westlands apartments.
  • Kilimani: A vibrant, central hub perfect for foodies and those wanting a mix of culture and urban convenience.
  • Karen: Lush, green, and peaceful. Ideal for nature lovers visiting the Giraffe Centre or Karen Blixen Museum.

2. Must-Visit Attractions

  • Nairobi National Park: Spot lions and rhinos against the city skyline. Pro Tip: Go for an early morning game drive for the best sightings.
  • David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust: Meet rescued baby elephants during their public feeding hour (11 AM – 12 PM).
  • Karura Forest: Perfect for a morning hike or bike ride to see the waterfalls.
  • Maasai Market: The go-to spot for authentic Kenyan crafts and jewelry.

3. Dining & Nightlife Highlights

Nairobi’s culinary scene is booming in 2026:

  • Best Coffee: Stop by Ora Cafe in Westlands for a great brew and a cozy atmosphere.
  • Fine Dining: Experience Cultiva, a world-renowned farm-to-table restaurant.
  • Rooftop Vibes: For sunset cocktails, head to Hero Bar or The Alchemist in Westlands.

4. Travel Tips for a Smooth Trip

  • Getting Around: Use Uber or Bolt for reliable transport.
  • Connectivity: Get a Safaricom SIM card at the airport for the best network coverage.
  • Stay Secure: Always book your stays through a trusted partner like Jirani Airbnbs to ensure 24/7 support and vetted properties.
